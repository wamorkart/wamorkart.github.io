I'm a particle physicist and postdoctoral researcher at Stanford University, working at the intersection of machine learning and experimental high-energy physics.

As a member of the ATLAS collaboration, I develop and apply ML methods to study data from the ATLAS experiment at the Large Hadron Collider. Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher in the ATLAS group at Argonne National Laboratory (2021–2024), where I performed precision measurements of the Standard Model and contributed to the ATLAS Tracker upgrade for the High Luminosity LHC.

I earned my Ph.D. from Northeastern University under Prof. Toyoko Orimoto, leading the first-ever CMS search for exotic Higgs boson decays into light pseudoscalars via photons. I'm always happy to collaborate—feel free to reach out!
